YOUR VISIT. YOUR CHOICE.

A little clarity about your details.

This website offers a local order bag, a WhatsApp order draft, and table requests. Here is how these features use information.

Your order bag.

Dish selections, quantities, spice choices and extras are saved in your browser so you can return to your bag. Names and kitchen notes are not saved in that local bag. Reviewing an order asks this website’s server to validate current prices and availability; it does not store an order or take payment.

Opening WhatsApp shares the prepared message with WhatsApp. You decide whether to send it there. WhatsApp applies its own privacy terms. A draft is not a confirmed restaurant order.

A table for your people.

Submitting a table request stores your name, contact number, optional email and notes, date, time and party size on the restaurant’s server. The owner uses these details to review and respond to your request. A table is confirmed only after owner approval.

Reservation records are retained for up to 90 days from submission. The owner can delete them sooner. Your private status link reveals the request’s date, time, party size and status to anyone who has the link, so keep it private. The link does not display your contact details. The most recent link is also kept in the browser’s session storage.

Help us make it better.

Optional first-party measurements start only after you choose “Allow measurements.” They count page visits, menu interactions, bag additions, call clicks, table-request openings and WhatsApp handoffs, plus browser timing and layout-shift measurements. They contain no names, phone numbers, booking details or order notes.

A random identifier groups measurements within the current page visit. We do not use advertising cookies, cross-site tracking or third-party analytics. Measurements are retained for up to 30 days, subject to storage limits. Your preference is saved in your browser; change it using “Measurement preferences” in the website footer. Disabling measurements stops future collection. The owner can clear stored measurements.

Keeping the house in order.

Owner access uses a password hash stored on the server and an HttpOnly session cookie that expires after eight hours. IP addresses are held temporarily in server memory for abuse prevention. Hosting providers may also maintain access logs under their own settings. Fonts and animation libraries load from their listed providers, which receive normal network requests.

The basic static version does not collect reservations or analytics. If an external booking service is configured, that service’s policies apply when you follow its link.
