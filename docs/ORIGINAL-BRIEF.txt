1. Concept
Brand placeholder: Mr. Biriyaniwala (Adelaide). 
The page is dark, warm and appetising: a near
black room lit by saffron and copper, with steam 
and embers everywhere. It opens on a pinned 
hero that serves eight dishes one after another 
as you scroll; each dish starts whole and 
explodes into its ingredients under your thumb. 
Then it tells the method — the dum: marinate, 
parboil, layer, seal the handi with dough, lift the 
lid at the table — and moves through the spices, 
the menu, the tandoor and the visit. Tone of 
voice: proud, specific and a little funny 
("Leftovers are a compliment. Containers are on 
the house.").
No text or logos in any image or video. Every 
word is HTML, so the restaurant can be renamed 
in the markup without regenerating a single 
asset. The asset prompts (second section of 
this pack) follow the same rule.

devices lose the custom cursor.
No console errors; no layout shift while 
media loads.
